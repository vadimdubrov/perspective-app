Perspective Principles

Lesson 01

1-Point Perspective

Uses 1 central VP on the Horizon Line. Front faces remain parallel to the picture plane, and depth lines converge straight to VP1.

Lesson 02

2-Point Perspective

Uses 2 VPs on the Horizon Line (VP1 & VP2). Vertical edges stay perpendicular to the horizon, while left and right depth lines converge to their respective VPs.

Lesson 03

3-Point Perspective

Uses 3 VPs. VP1 and VP2 lie on the Horizon Line, and VP3 controls vertical perspective convergence (Zenith when looking up, Nadir when looking down).
