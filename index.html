Baryshnikov Course

Lesson 01

The Picture Plane

Imagine a transparent glass window between your eye and the scene. Everything you draw lives on this plane. The frame you see is your picture plane — the boundary of your canvas.

Lesson 02

Build with simple forms

Every complex scene is built from cubes, spheres, cylinders, pyramids and prisms. Master these, and you master everything.

Lesson 03

Distance Points (D1/D2)

Use D1 and D2 to measure true depth and construct accurate circles in perspective — a cornerstone of Baryshnikov's method.
